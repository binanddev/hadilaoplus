# Secret Hitler artwork

Original game: Mike Boxleiter, Tommy Maranges and Mac Schubert.
Source: https://www.secrethitler.com/
License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The user-supplied grayscale Print & Play PDF supplied role, party, ballot, policy,
draw/discard, office placard and board images. Board halves on pages 10–13 were
joined, rotated upright and trimmed at their printed cut lines. Original art and
lettering are retained; images are encoded as WebP. `card-back.svg` is a new vector
pattern for the web adaptation. Vietnamese labels, the 3D room, seating choreography
and execution presentation are new adaptation work, shared under the same license.
Full attribution and changes: docs/ATTRIBUTION.md in the source repository.
